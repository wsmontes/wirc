Wirc brings all your content together in one place — RSS feeds, YouTube channels, podcasts, Mastodon, and IRC chat. No algorithm. No ads. No tracking. Just the stuff you asked for, from the places you chose.

FEATURES:
- 200+ curated sources across tech, news, podcasts, and video
- Mixed timeline with source diversity — never see 10 podcasts in a row
- Full multi-server IRC client built in
- Save articles for later with bookmarks
- Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver support
- Share Extension: send any URL to Wirc
- Home Screen Widget: see latest posts at a glance
- 100% local storage — your data never leaves your device
